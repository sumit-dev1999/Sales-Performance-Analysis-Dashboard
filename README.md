## 2. Project Overview

This project is a Microsoft Excel Data Analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance, product profitability, regional sales, and salesperson performance using a 30-row sales dataset.

The project uses Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI metrics, and an interactive dashboard to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 4. Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales transactions and understand business performance using Excel.

This analysis helps answer important business questions:

- How much revenue did the business generate?
- How much did the business spend on product costs?
- What is the total profit?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which region performs the best?
- Which salesperson generates the highest revenue?
- Which payment method is most commonly used?
- Which category contributes the most to sales?
- What is the overall profit margin?

## 5. Dataset Description

The dataset contains 30 sales transactions with the following columns:

| Column Name | Description |
|---|---|
| Order_ID | Unique transaction ID |
| Order_Date | Date of the order |
| Product | Product name |
| Category | Product category |
| Region | Sales region |
| Salesperson | Employee responsible for the sale |
| Quantity | Number of units sold |
| Unit_Cost | Cost per unit |
| Unit_Selling_Price | Selling price per unit |
| Total_Cost | Total cost of products sold |
| Total_Sales | Total revenue generated |
| GST_Rate | Applicable GST rate |
| Profit | Profit earned on the order |
| Payment_Mode | Customer payment method |

## 8. Pivot Table Analysis

### 8.1 Payment Mode-wise Sales

| Payment Mode | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| UPI | ₹3,35,550 |
| Bank Transfer | ₹2,08,000 |
| Card | ₹2,05,350 |
| Cash | ₹88,750 |

**Analysis:** UPI is the highest revenue-generating payment mode, contributing approximately 40% of total sales.

### 8.2 Salesperson-wise Sales

| Salesperson | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Rahul | ₹4,02,150 |
| Priya | ₹1,60,750 |
| Amit | ₹1,45,400 |
| Neha | ₹1,29,350 |

**Analysis:** Rahul generated the highest sales revenue among the four salespeople.

### 8.3 Region-wise Sales

| Region | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| West | ₹4,02,150 |
| South | ₹1,60,750 |
| North | ₹1,45,400 |
| East | ₹1,29,350 |

**Analysis:** The West region contributed the largest share of revenue, accounting for approximately 48% of total sales.

### 8.4 Product-wise Sales

| Product | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Laptop | ₹2,70,000 |
| Smartphone | ₹1,76,000 |
| Monitor | ₹1,03,500 |
| Tablet | ₹97,500 |
| Printer | ₹54,000 |

**Analysis:** Laptop sales generated the highest revenue among the products displayed in the Top 5 Product Sales analysis.

### 8.5 Category-wise Sales

| Category | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Electronics | ₹7,29,000 |
| Accessories | ₹1,08,650 |

**Analysis:** Electronics generated the majority of sales revenue, contributing approximately 87% of total sales.

## 10. Key Business Insights

1. **Strong Revenue Performance:** Total sales reached ₹8,37,650 across 30 transactions.
2. **Profitability:** The business generated ₹2,26,350 in profit, with a profit margin of approximately 27%.
3. **Product Performance:** Laptops were the highest-selling product by revenue among the products shown in the Top 5 chart.
4. **Regional Performance:** The West region generated the highest sales, contributing approximately 48% of total revenue.
5. **Salesperson Performance:** Rahul generated the highest sales revenue of ₹4,02,150.
6. **Payment Preference:** UPI was the most popular payment mode by revenue, contributing approximately 40% of total sales.
7. **Category Performance:** Electronics contributed approximately 87% of total sales, making it the dominant category.
